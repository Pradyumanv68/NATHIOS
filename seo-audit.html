    WHAT WE CHECK

    Seven areas, up to 100 pages, one score.

    01

    Technical health

    HTTPS, broken pages, redirect chains, canonical tags, mobile setup, robots.txt and pages Google can’t reach.

    02

    On-page SEO

    Titles, meta descriptions, headings, duplicate tags, URL format and how deep pages are buried.

    03

    Content

    Thin and duplicate pages, readability, and whether each title matches what the page actually says.

    04

    Schema

    The structured data that tells Google and AI tools who you are, what you sell and how pages relate.

    05

    Mobile speed

    A real Google Lighthouse test of your home page on a phone: loading, interaction and layout shifts.

    06

    AI search readiness

    Whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI can read your site, plus llms.txt, FAQ markup and brand signals.

    07

    Images

    Missing alt text, oversized files, old formats, broken images and missing dimensions.
