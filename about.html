Founded by builders, not sellers

We build the products we'd want to use ourselves.

Nathios started because too many small businesses were quoted six-month timelines and vague budgets for things that should take weeks. We fix that with fixed scopes, weekly demos and a small senior team on every project.

From a two-person side project to a full studio.

Nathios began as a freelance duo building websites for local shops. Clients kept asking for the same three things: a website, an app to go with it, and something to answer customer questions after hours. So we built a team around exactly that.

Today we're a distributed studio of designers, engineers and AI specialists who ship in fixed sprints, price everything up front, and hand over full ownership of the code at the end.

What guides how we work.

A few working principles that show up in every project, not just the pitch deck.

Fixed scope

Every plan states exactly what's included and what it costs, so the number you agree to is the number you pay.

Weekly visibility

You see a working build every week, not a status update. If something's off, we fix it before it compounds.

Full ownership

Source code, design files and accounts transfer to you at launch. There's no lock-in to keep us in the loop.

Senior team

You work with the people actually building your product, not a rotating account manager relay.

Small and fast

We stay a lean team on purpose — enough to move quickly, small enough that nothing gets lost.

Built to grow

Every product we ship is designed for the next feature, not just the one you asked for first.
