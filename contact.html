Questions we hear most.

How long does a project take?

A five-page website takes about two weeks. A mobile app MVP takes around six weeks. We give you an exact schedule after the discovery step, and you see progress every week.

Do I own the code and the design?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the source code, design files and accounts are transferred to you. There is no lock-in.

Can one app run on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. We build with Flutter or React Native, so a single codebase covers both platforms. That keeps cost and maintenance lower than building two separate apps.

How do payments work?

We split the project into three payments: half to start, 30% when the design is approved and 20% at launch. Care plans are billed monthly.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes. After that you can move to a monthly care plan for updates, backups, monitoring and small changes, or hire us only when you need something new.

What will the AI chatbot know?

Only what you give it: your website pages, FAQs, product lists and documents. It hands the conversation to your team when it is unsure, and you get a monthly report of what customers asked.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with teams around the world, meet you on video calls in your time zone and invoice in INR or USD.
